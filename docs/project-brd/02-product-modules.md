# Product Modules

## Core platform modules
1. Dashboard — KPIs, trends, upcoming work and shortcuts.
2. Appointments — calendar, list, status, services, staff assignment, duration, price and analytics.
3. Clients — directory, profiles, history, segmentation, wallet and points.
4. Services — categories, service definitions, prices and duration.
5. Staff — profiles, roles, availability, attendance, performance, tips, wages and commissions.
6. Sales / POS — service/product transactions, discounts, taxes where enabled, payments and tips.
7. Inventory — products, stock, cost and thresholds.
8. Loyalty — packages, memberships, gift cards, wallet and points.
9. Finance — payments, expenses and management reports.
10. Reports — central report hub reusing module report logic.
11. Settings — organization and module configuration.
